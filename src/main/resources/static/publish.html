发布结构化研究成果 Publish Structured Research Work

把普通帖子升级为可检索、可归档、可讨论的开放研究条目，便于后续发现、评价与知识积累。 Turn a normal post into a searchable, archivable, and discussable open research entry for later discovery, evaluation, and reuse.
建议包含的信息Recommended Information
1
清晰标题 + 摘要，说明研究问题、方法与核心结果。A clear title and abstract explaining the research problem, method, and outcomes.
2
作者、机构、领域、关键词，方便搜索和聚类。Authors, institution, area, and keywords for better discovery and clustering.
3
补充引用、资源链接和附件，帮助他人复现或继续研究。References, resource links, and attachments help others verify and reuse the work.

作者完善工作流Author Improvement Workflow
  • 先在“我的研究档案”中识别优先改进条目。First identify priority entries in My Research Archive.
  • 在此页补充 DOI、引用、附件与结构化元数据。Then complete DOI, references, attachments, and structured metadata here.
  • 保存后返回“研究成果管理”或“我的研究档案”继续复查。After saving, return to Research Works or My Research Archive for follow-up review.

🧭 成果分类建议Suggested Work Categories

当前后端支持灵活文本字段，你可以把研究成果组织为论文、报告、项目输出、数据集说明或实验记录。 The current backend supports flexible text fields, so you can structure the work as a paper, report, project output, dataset note, or experiment record.
论文 / 预印本Paper / Preprint
适合正式研究成果、课程论文、投递中的文章。Best for formal research outputs, coursework papers, or submissions in progress.
项目 / 数据集Project / Dataset
适合代码仓库、实验平台、模型、数据集及其说明。Best for code repositories, platforms, models, datasets, and documentation.
报告 / 记录Report / Note
适合研究日志、实验记录、阶段总结和内部知识沉淀。Best for research logs, experiment notes, milestone reports, and internal knowledge capture.
建议把标题写成可被搜索和引用的形式。Write the title in a form that is easy to search and cite.
建议按“问题 - 方法 - 结果 - 价值”结构书写，更利于读者理解与后续评价。A “problem - method - result - value” structure makes the work easier to understand and evaluate.

🧾 研究条目元数据Research Entry Metadata

这些字段会影响首页展示、搜索筛选、详情页结构化展示和完整度评估。 These fields affect homepage cards, search filters, structured detail display, and completeness scoring.
可填写多个作者或团队名称，建议用逗号分隔。You can enter multiple authors or a team name, preferably separated by commas.
如果不是正式发表内容，也可以填写实验室分享、课程展示或项目演示。If not formally published, you can enter a lab sharing event, class presentation, or project demo.
如没有 DOI，可以留空，或填写 arXiv 编号、仓库编号、项目唯一标识。If there is no DOI, leave it blank or use an arXiv ID, repository ID, or other persistent identifier.

🔗 参考资料与项目资源References and Project Resources

建议至少给出关键文献或数据来源，方便论证依据和后续查证。Adding key references or data sources helps justify the work and support verification.
多个链接可换行输入；建议以 http(s):// 开头，增强复现性与传播性。Use one line per link if needed; starting with http(s):// improves reproducibility and sharing.

🏷️ 研究标签Research Tags

标签会影响搜索命中、主题聚类与首页发现流推荐。 Tags affect search hits, thematic clustering, and discovery feed recommendations.

💡 填写建议Authoring Tips

如果你的条目稍后还会继续补充，可以先完成标题、摘要、作者、领域和关键词，再逐步完善 DOI、引用、附件与资源链接。 If your entry will keep evolving, start with title, abstract, authors, area, and keywords first, then gradually enrich DOI, references, attachments, and project links.
